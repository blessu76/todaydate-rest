Each Scene Gets Its Own Background and Expressions

No two scenes in 오늘 어때 share the same picture. Here is how each scene gets its own background, time of day and character expressions.

In 오늘 어때, no two scenes use the same picture. A cafe at night looks different from a cafe at noon. A park in the snow is not a park in the rain. Each scene has its own background, and its characters change expression as the story moves.

How a scene is set

Every scene records a few things: its season, its weather, the time of day, and a background key. The background key tells the art which image to draw. A scene about rain uses a rain background. A night scene uses a darker palette.

Expressions follow the choice

The characters react to what you pick. A warm answer brings a smile. A teasing answer brings a grin. A careless one brings a pause. The reaction is drawn from a small set for each character, so the expressions stay consistent from scene to scene.

Why it matters

A player who sees the same room five times in a round stops reading the scene. Changing the background and the expression gives each choice a place in the story, even before the words are read.
