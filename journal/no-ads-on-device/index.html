No Ads, and Your Records Stay on Your Device

오늘 어때 has no ads and no in-app purchases that change a scene's outcome. Your records stay on the phone you play on.

오늘 어때 has no ads. It does not show ads between rounds, and it does not offer rewards for watching them. Hints, rewinds and retries are not sold either, because they would change how a round plays.

What you can buy

The only purchase is a scene pack. A pack adds scenes. It does not change the rules, the scoring or the endings.

Where your records live

Your collection, the scene packs you own and your progress are stored on the device you play on. We do not keep a copy of your play history on a server. If you delete the app, those records go with it.

Why we chose this

A date game should not follow you around the internet. Keeping the records on your phone is simpler, and it means we have less to protect. We would rather make a small, clear game than a large one that needs to watch you to pay for itself.
