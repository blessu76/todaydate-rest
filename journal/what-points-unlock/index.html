What points unlock

The core game is free. Use points to open scene packs and character stories.

오늘 어때 has no ads. You can start choosing replies and reading how a scene plays out in the free game.

Optional extras

Points open scene packs and character stories. The first time you open the app, you get enough points for one scene pack. When point packs are available from your app store, you can buy more points in the app.
