## What the last line brings back

While a story is still going, your attention is on what will happen next. A remark can have one sound when you first read it and another when you get to the end. Take your time with the last line on the ending screen. It can remind you why you wanted the conversation to go on a little longer, or make you smile at something that felt quite serious on the way there.

Think about what you remember after a real afternoon out. You might recall where you stopped or an offhand remark that made you laugh. Details like these can give you a way to place the rest of the day. The name of an ending can be a starting point for remembering a date in the game. What you recall around it will be your own.

## Looking through the Ending Collection

When a story has been on your mind, look in the Ending Collection for the name of the ending you reached. You may remember the setup clearly and have forgotten the mood. You may remember a reaction and need a moment to place the conversation that led there. The name can give you a place to begin in either case.

Different ending names can bring different details to mind. One can bring back a particular conversation. Another might remind you of how you felt as you read. When you look again on a different day, you might notice a name that had passed you by and remember the story that went with it.

## Sending a result card to a friend

You can share a result card with a friend from the ending screen. If you want to tell them about the date, consider what made that ending stick. You could mention that the conversation took a turn you didn’t expect. You could tell them what you heard in the last line. A little context gives your friend something to ask about.

A result card gives your friend a glimpse of how the date ended and leaves the rest to their imagination. You can tell them more another time. You might also be happy to spend a moment with the last line on the ending screen and leave it there.

The next time you finish a date, see which moment you remember later. It may be the one you thought about during the scene, or a quieter line that came back to you afterward. When the ending name brings the moment to mind, give yourself some time to remember what you liked.
