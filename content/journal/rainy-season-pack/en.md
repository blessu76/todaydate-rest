오늘 어때 has a winter scene pack. In Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese, winter does not fit. Snow, scarves and frost are not part of most people's year there. So we did not translate the winter scenes. We gave those three languages a rainy-season pack.

## What changes

The rainy-season pack has four scenes. A sudden downpour at a bus stop. A warm soup place on a wet evening. A walk along a street that has just stopped raining. A drink under a shelter while the rain falls.

Each one uses the same five-choice structure as the rest of the game, so the scoring works the same way. Only the setting changes.

## What stays the same

The pack appears only in Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese. The other nine languages keep the winter pack. The price tier matches the winter pack, so a player in those three languages sees the same choice in the same place in the store.

We did not use winter scenes with local details pasted on top. A rainy season of its own felt more honest than a winter that had been moved.
