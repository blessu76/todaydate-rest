Privacy Policy

What information 오늘 어때 handles, explained plainly.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Information we collect

오늘 어때 does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers. You do not need to create an account or sign in.

Server transmission

Your scene choices and postcard content are handled only on your device and are not sent to a server.

Ads and analytics

The app shows no ads and includes no usage analytics or tracking tools.

In-app purchases

Scene packs and decorations unlock with points bought in the app. Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play, and we never receive your payment information. Because there are no servers or accounts, your point balance is stored only on your device. If you delete the app or change devices, your balance may be lost.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page first. For questions, please use the Support page.
