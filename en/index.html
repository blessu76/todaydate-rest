Frequently asked questions

Are there ads or purchases?

No ads. The core game is free. Scene packs and decorations are optional and unlock with points, which you can buy in the app. Your point balance is stored only on your device, and payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

Which languages are available?

11 languages. You can change the language and theme in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-in is needed. Scenes and postcards are handled on your device.
